# Persona

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals and organization administrators publish a profile and maintain one public Persona chatbot. Visitors ask questions on Persona or a connected external website. Super Admin manages customers and platform operations.

## Product Purpose

Help people and organizations share approved knowledge through their own AI persona, improving its coverage by uploading documents and publishing updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One public Persona and one private Admin Assistant per workspace. Existing individual features and URLs remain supported. External integrations keep secret API keys in customer backends. Organization onboarding changes profile information and naming while retaining the core workflow.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the established green-and-white interface. User-requested refinements: clear wording, comfortable text sizes, attention to detail, skeleton loading, measured progress, responsive forms and recoverable failures.

## Product Principles

- Separate human login identity from the workspace being represented.
- Publish reviewed knowledge and respect current visibility.
- Keep slow operations understandable and preserve user input.
- Show operational evidence without inventing health or success.
